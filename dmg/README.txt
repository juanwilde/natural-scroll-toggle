Natural Scroll Toggle
=====================

Menu bar toggle for macOS Natural Scrolling, with automatic mode:
natural scrolling on the trackpad, classic scrolling with a mouse.

1. Double-click "Install.command"  (it installs the app and sets it to
   start at login; it may ask to allow it in
   System Settings > Privacy & Security).

2. When macOS asks, grant Accessibility permission to NaturalScrollToggle
   (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility). Without it the
   scroll change is saved but not applied until apps restart.

3. Look for the arrows icon in the menu bar, next to the clock:
   green = natural scrolling on, red = off.
   Connect/disconnect a mouse and it switches by itself.

Uninstall: delete NaturalScrollToggle.app from ~/Applications and
com.wilde.naturalscrolltoggle.plist from ~/Library/LaunchAgents.

Full source and updates: https://github.com/juanwilde/natural-scroll-toggle
